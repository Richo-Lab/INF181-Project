# ShopSmart IS: an IS Guide for Small Retail Businesses

A digital information systems (IS) resource for owners and staff of small South African retail businesses. It explains how information systems can help a growing business manage inventory, customer data, and finances, in plain language and with practical guidance.

## About this project

This website was built for **INF1781 (Information Systems)** at Belgium Campus iTversity as a group project. We took the role of IS consultants advising a small South African retail business that currently relies on spreadsheets, paper records, and manual processes, and wants to grow.

## Who it's for

Small business owners and staff with limited IT knowledge who want to understand how information systems can support their business.

## What the site covers

1. Data and information management (including dashboards and information quality)
2. Data ethics, privacy, and POPIA compliance
3. AI and decision support
4. Enterprise systems and integration
5. Adapting and customising information systems to fit the business

## Running it locally

1. Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/Richo-Lab/INF181-Project.git`
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
No installation or build step is needed.
